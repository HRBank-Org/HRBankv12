<product_requirements>
The HR Bank is a multi-role workforce management platform connecting **Workforce**, **Employers**, **Institutions**, and **Admins**.

**Core Functionality:**
*   **Role-Based Dashboards & Settings:** Each user role has a personalized dashboard and settings page to manage their specific profile information (name, logo/photo, contact details, password).
*   **Document Management System:** A centralized system for users to upload and manage documents.
    *   Requirements are role-specific (e.g., Work Permit for Workforce, Business Registration for Employers).
    *   Some documents are file uploads (ID, permits), while others are text-based numbers (BN, GST/HST).
    *   Documents with expiry dates trigger automated email reminders via SendGrid 7 days before expiry.
    *   User accounts are restricted (can log in but cannot perform key actions) if required documents expire.

**Institution-Specific Features:**
*   **Credential Management:** Institutions can create and manage Classes and Class Templates.
*   **Digital Credential Issuance:** Institutions can issue mock blockchain-based credentials to students (Workforce users).
*   **Verification Workflow:** Institutions can review and approve/reject verification requests submitted by Workforce users for credentials they have uploaded.
*   **Analytics Dashboard:** A dedicated dashboard provides metrics on issued certifications and ongoing classes.
</product_requirements>
