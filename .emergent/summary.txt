<product_requirements>
The project is to build HR Bank, a multi-tenant workforce management platform for four user types: **Workforce**, **Employers**, **Institutions**, and **Admins**.

The platform's core requirements, developed iteratively, include:
1.  **Multi-User System**: Separate, branded experiences for Workforce (blue), Employers (orange), and Institutions (black), with full authentication and role-specific dashboards.
2.  **Scheduling**: A Google Calendar-style system for workers to set availability and for employers to schedule shifts.
3.  **Recruitment & Onboarding**: An invitation system for employers to invite new workers to jobs/shifts, guiding them through account creation.
4.  **Workforce Lifecycle Management**: A system to track the employment relationship between workers and employers, including employment history and managing offboarding/termination.
5.  **Compliance**: A mandatory, user-type-specific EULA acceptance flow. A document management system where users upload documents (e.g., ID, business registration) that require admin approval, with validity tracking and expiry notifications.
6.  **Admin Hierarchy**: A comprehensive admin portal () with a Super Admin role capable of creating other admins and assigning them to specific geographic zones for localized management.
7.  **Communication**: Integrated chat and notification features accessible to all user types.
</product_requirements>
