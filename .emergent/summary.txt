PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:
- **Phase 1: Workforce Tab:** A central place for workforce management, including recruiting, an inventory of all workers presented in cards with their details (photo, occupation, ratings, skills), and a visualization of workforce distribution across different workplaces.

- **Phase 4: Finances Tab:** A bundle for financial and attendance-related features, including a live attendance view, timesheets, payroll information, and modern attendance methods like QR code scanning and geofencing.
