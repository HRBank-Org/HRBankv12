<product_requirements>
The HR Bank is a multi-role workforce management platform designed to connect a **Workforce** (casual employees), **Employers**, **Institutions**, and **Admins** within the Canadian legal framework.

**Core Functionality:**
*   **Role-Based Access:** Each user role has a personalized dashboard and login portal, with functionality tailored to their needs.
*   **Document Management:** A centralized system for users to upload and manage role-specific documents (e.g., Work Permits, Business Licenses). The system tracks expiry dates and sends automated email reminders.
*   **Legal & Payroll Compliance (Canada):**
    *   Enforces correct employee classification (T4) during employer onboarding.
    *   Requires and verifies WSIB coverage before employers can post shifts.
    *   Tracks and calculates Employment Standards Act (ESA) entitlements, including vacation pay (4%), public holiday pay, and overtime (>44 hrs/week).
    *   Provides payroll export functionality for systems like ADP and Ceridian.
*   **Attendance System:** Geolocation-based clock-in/out for workers, with real-time attendance tracking for employers and timesheet generation.
*   **Credentialing System:** Institutions can create class templates, issue mock blockchain credentials to students (Workforce), and manage a queue for credential verification requests.
*   **PWA & Subdomains:** The application is set up to be deployed with dedicated Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) for Employer and Workforce roles, accessible via unique subdomains (e.g., ).
</product_requirements>
