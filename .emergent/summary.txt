The HR Bank is a multi-role workforce management platform designed to connect a **Workforce**, **Employers**, **Institutions**, and **Admins** in the Canadian market.

**Core functionality includes:**
*   **Role-Based Portals:** Each user role has a dedicated login portal accessible via a unique subdomain (e.g., , ).
*   **Compliance:** The system enforces Ontario's labor laws, including minimum wage, T4 classification, and WSIB coverage.
*   **AI Onboarding Assistant (Emma):** A new conversational AI assistant guides users through profile creation, explains platform benefits, and collects necessary compliance documents (e.g., ID, resume) via chat. Emma is branded according to the user's role and is persistently available.
*   **Job Matching Engine:** Employers can post available roles to a matching engine. The system then suggests suitable candidates from the workforce based on skills, certifications, and geographic proximity. Employers can invite candidates to an in-app video interview or send a direct job offer.
*   **Workforce Dashboard:** Workers can manage their profile, view a streamlined dashboard with Financial, My Shifts, and Career Growth tabs, search for jobs, and manage applications and interviews.
