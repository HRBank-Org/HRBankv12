**Key Technical Concepts**
-   **Dependency-Aware Lifecycle Management:** Implementing backend checks to prevent actions (like deactivating a workplace) if dependent entities (active shifts, assigned workers) exist, and notifying the user.
-   **Contextual Onboarding UI:** Dynamically rendering UI elements (banners, disabled buttons) to guide new users through required setup steps before they can access a feature.
-   **National Compliance Engine:** A system where business rules (minimum wage, certifications) are dynamically applied based on geographical location (province), ensuring legal compliance across different regions.
-   **Component Consolidation:** Improving code maintainability by removing legacy components () and unifying the UX around a single, modern component ().
